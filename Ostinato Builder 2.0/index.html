Easy mode

Press the beat-dot button beside Play until it reads EASY, and every beat offers a row of coloured circles instead of its dots. Each circle writes a whole rhythm in one tap, and tapping the lit one again turns it into a rest. The longer notes only appear on the beats they can start on. Choose how many circles there are, and what each one writes.

How this works

An ostinato is a short pattern that repeats. Each instrument gets its own line, and the lines play together, round and round.

  • Tap a dot to turn that part of the beat into a sound or a rest. The notes redraw themselves.
  • +
    Split a beat into smaller notes. Press − to go back.
  • 🔗
    Join two beats with the chain between them, for half and whole notes.
  • %
    Repeat a beat or a bar: point at an empty beat or a bar line and tap the repeat mark to copy the one before it.
  • 🥁
    Change an instrument by tapping its picture. The speaker on the picture mutes that line, and Add, under the last picture, brings in another.
  • ＋
    Set the length with the bar counter on the toolbar, or the + and × where the music ends.
  • ⛶
    Present hides every control and fills the screen. Press Esc to come back.
  • ␣
    Spacebar starts and stops the music.
