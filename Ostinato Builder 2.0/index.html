Easy mode

Press the dots button beside Play until it reads EASY, and every beat offers a row of coloured circles instead of its dots. Each circle writes a whole rhythm in one tap, and tapping the lit one again turns it into a rest. The longer notes only appear on the beats they can start on. Choose how many circles there are, and what each one writes.
