Reference pitch

Nearly everyone tunes to A = 440 Hz. Some orchestras tune a little higher. Old instruments are often tuned lower.

How close is close enough?

Cents are tiny steps in pitch: 100 cents make one semitone. Beginners can aim for 10 cents. Advanced players aim for 3.
