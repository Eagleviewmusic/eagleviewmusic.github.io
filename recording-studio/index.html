Background

How it works

Pick a track with its round button, then play the instrument below it. Press to record onto that track while the others play. A new take replaces what the track had, from where it started to where you stopped — Undo brings it back. Space plays and stops.

Line up the timing

Speakers and headphones each take a moment to play a sound. Tap along with the click and the studio learns how long yours take, so a recording lands right on the beat.
