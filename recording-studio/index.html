Background

How it works

Pick a track with its round button, then play the instrument below it. Press to record onto that track while the others play. Each take is a part on its track: drag its middle to move it, an end to size it, and double-click a track to open its notes. A track's loop button makes its parts repeat when pulled out. Space plays and stops; − and + zoom.

Line up the timing

Speakers and headphones each take a moment to play a sound. Tap along with the click and the studio learns how long yours take, so a recording lands right on the beat.
