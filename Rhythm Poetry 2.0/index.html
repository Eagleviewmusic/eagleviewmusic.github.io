Easy mode

Press the beat-dot button beside Play until it reads EASY, and every beat offers a row of coloured circles instead of its dots. Each circle writes a whole rhythm in one tap, and tapping the lit one again turns it into a rest. The longer notes only appear on the beats they can start on. Choose how many circles there are, and what each one writes.

How this works

  • Tap a dot to turn that half-beat into a sound or a rest. The notation above redraws itself.
  • +
    Split a beat into four sixteenths. Press − to go back to two.
  • 🔗
    Link two beats to write dotted rhythms and ties across the beat.
  • ▸
    Tap any measure to mark where playback should begin — handy for drilling one spot. Tap it again to clear it.
  • ＋
    Add or remove measures with the + and × at the end of the last line.
  • ⛶
    Present hides every control and fills the screen. Press Esc to come back.
  • ␣
    Spacebar starts and pauses playback. Tapping the background does the same.
