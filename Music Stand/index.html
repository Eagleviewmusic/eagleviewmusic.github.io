How this works

The Music Stand plays a poem from Rhythm Poetry and an ostinato from Ostinato Builder together, on one beat.

  • ♪
    Choose the music by tapping the name at the top of each score. Pick from that app’s Library, or paste a link.
  • ▶
    Play starts both together. ⟲ Loop goes round again when the poem ends.
  • 🎚
    Mixer has the count-in, an intro where the ostinato plays alone first, and every voice on or off. Tap an instrument’s picture to change it.
  • 👁
    View sets side by side or stacked, which score comes first, and their sizes. Drag the line between the scores to resize; double-click it to let the stand choose.
  • ✎
    Edit the scores (in View) lets you change either score right here. Changes stay on the stand, and the apps are not touched.
  • 🔊
    The eye and speaker on each score set how it is shown, and mute it.
  • 📚
    Library saves the pair as an arrangement and shares it by link.
  • ⛶
    Present shows just the two scores, full screen. Press Esc to come back.
  • ␣
    Spacebar starts and stops the music.
