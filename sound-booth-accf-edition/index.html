About Sound Booth-ACCF Edition

Sound Booth-ACCF Edition is a simple multi-track recorder for one job: recording a clean vocal line while you sing along to an accompaniment.

It records your microphone exactly as it arrives. It adds no effects, and it asks your device to turn off its own noise removal, echo cancellation and automatic volume, so nothing squeezes or colours your voice. Your recordings stay uncompressed until you export them as an MP3.

Load an ACCF Song or add your own accompaniment, put on headphones and record. Each take lines up with the music by itself, and every exported file starts at 0:00, so the parts drop straight into place.

Everything stays on this computer. Nothing is ever uploaded.

    Headphones and timing

    Sing about a hand’s width from the microphone, a little off to the side. Leave one ear uncovered so you can hear yourself. This app can’t change your microphone’s hardware level, so if it’s too loud or too quiet, move, or change the input volume in your computer’s sound settings. It adds no effects and doesn’t clean up your sound, so a quiet room makes the biggest difference.
