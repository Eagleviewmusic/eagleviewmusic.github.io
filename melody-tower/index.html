About Song Writer

Created by Nathan Walby, Song Writer is an intuitive web app designed to help students compose and perform music using accessible graphic notation. Instead of getting bogged down by complex traditional scoring, students can focus purely on melody, rhythm, and musical exploration.

Key Features

▶
Interactive Playback: Play along in real time using on-screen buttons or your keyboard’s left/right arrow keys—tap in rhythm to bring your melody to life.
🎨
Full Creative Control: Compose original tunes, edit existing songs, and transpose to different keys with ease.
🔗
Easy Sharing & Printing: Share creations instantly via direct links or export high-quality images ready for printing.
📁
Offline Song Library: Organize, maintain, and back up your entire song catalog right on your computer.

Private, Secure & Frictionless

🔒
No Accounts Required: Jump straight into creating—no logins, passwords, or personal data collection.
💾
100% Local Storage: Your work stays yours. Projects are saved directly in your browser's local storage or exported as lightweight JSON files to reload anytime.
