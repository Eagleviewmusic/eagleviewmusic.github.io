Librarian

Publish songs to every student

  1. Tick the songs you want students to have, and choose the book each one goes in. Books are what students see on the shelf.
  2. Download them — one small file each.
  3. Upload the files to the Teacher Library on GitHub. About a minute later, every student’s app has them.

Music Stand pairings go out whole: the poem and the ostinato travel inside the pairing, as they are in your apps when you publish, with the tempo, intro and sound. Students need only that one file. Edit either song later and the pairing shows Changed since you published.

Each piece takes its Layout Settings with it (EASY rhythms included), just as you last saved it — students get them locked for that piece.

Students take a book off the shelf in any app and its songs join their library as Shared songs: exactly as you made them, with Save my copy to keep their own changes. Putting the book back removes them; their own copies stay. Publish a song again after changing it and students get the new version.

  3. Press Commit changes. The library updates itself — nothing else to edit.
