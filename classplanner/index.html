      🍎✨ Welcome to ClassPlanner!

      Hey there! ClassPlanner was created by Nathan Walby, an elementary school music teacher at Eagle View Elementary, who needed a fast, flexible, and completely safe way to juggle multiple classes, seating arrangements, and grade records without fighting clunky software. Built by a teacher, for teachers, it’s designed to keep your classroom running smoothly so you can focus on what actually matters—teaching your students!

      🔒 100% Private, Safe, & Secure

      We know how serious student privacy is. That’s why ClassPlanner is built as a strictly local tool. None of your student data, attendance records, or grades ever leave your computer. There are no accounts to make, no servers tracking your information, and zero cloud databases. Everything stays saved right in your browser's local storage—making it completely FERPA-friendly and completely safe from third-party eyes. You can easily download a simple JSON backup file to move your gradebook between computers or keep a hard backup safely on your flash drive!

      🎨 Flexible Layouts for Real Classrooms

      Every classroom setup is different, and ClassPlanner lets you flex your space however you need:

      • Multiple Room Layouts: Switch instantly between a Garden of potted plants on the classroom shelves, traditional Rows (with option to divide down the center for aisles), vertical Lines, a customizable Circle dial, or Group Tables (halves, thirds, fourths, fifths, and sixths) with students seated all the way around each table. In Edit mode, drag a table's corners to size it, and click its Round / Square button to change its shape.
      • Easy Drag-and-Drop: Move students around with simple drag-and-drop seating controls. Drop a name between two others (or on the edge of a name card) and it slides in right there; drop it in the middle of a card to swap the two. Need to balance your groups? Hit the shuffle/balance feature to instantly mix up your seating.
      • Visual Roster Management: Track who is Present, Absent, or To Be Placed right from the sidebar. Single-click seats to mark students absent on the fly, or double-click to pull up a student name card to update names and initials!

      📋 Attendance & Gradebook Registers Built Right In

      • Live In-Chart Grading: Hit "Start" to launch a live grading session directly on your visual seating chart. Tap buttons right on student seats to mark quick checks (+), gold stars (★, tap + twice), minuses (−), or unsatisfactory (✕, tap − twice) during active lessons.
      • Attendance Garden: Choose the Garden layout and every student gets a mystery plant in a pot on the classroom shelves, one of ten: a flytrap, violet, daisy, rose, bellflower, dahlia, sunflower, tulip, lily or cactus. Plants grow through six stages, from an empty pot to full bloom, one stage for each class a student attends in a row (classes that meet daily grow more slowly; change the stages in Settings → Garden Growth). Everyone starts at stage 1 the day a class's garden first opens. An absence before full bloom loses the plant and brings a different kind; a full-bloom flower is kept until it's replanted. To fix a garden by hand, turn on Settings → Edit Garden: each click on a pot grows it one stage, and border flowers can be removed. Click a flower in full bloom to replant it into the flower bed outside the window, where the class's garden keeps every one; the student then starts a new pot with a different mystery plant.
      • Interactive Spreadsheets: Jump over to the Attendance or Grades views for a clean, frozen-header register view. Sort assessment columns by category themes—like Singing, Instruments, Movement, Culture, Theory, and Effort!
      • One-Click Camera Snapshots: Need a sub plan or a quick physical printout for your clipboard? Click the camera icon to snap high-resolution JPEG pictures of your seating charts, attendance logs, or grade registers in seconds—complete with a fun camera shutter sound!
      ClassPlanner gives you total control over your classroom organization—simple, private, and tailored for everyday teaching! 🚀
