The recorded instruments in Virtual Keyboard 2.0

Every note in these folders comes from two free sample libraries by
Versilian Studios, both released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0):
public domain, free for any use, no credit required. Credited here anyway.

  VCSL — Versilian Community Sample Library
  https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL
    grand-piano      Grand Piano, Steinway B (Sus, close mics, velocity layer 3)
    harpsichord      Harpsichord, English (Zuckermann kit, Normal)
    pipe-organ       Pipe Organ (Quiet)
    concert-harp     Concert Harp (mf)
    vibraphone       Vibraphone (soft mallets)
    concert-marimba  Marimba (Outrigger, medium)
    xylophone        Xylophone (medium mallets, ff)

  VSCO 2 CE — Versilian Studios Chamber Orchestra 2, Community Edition
  https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
    violins          Violin Section (sustain, vibrato)
    flute            Flute (sustain, no vibrato)
    trumpet          Trumpet (sustain)

What was changed (by _build/build-samples.mjs): a few notes per instrument
were kept (the keyboard bends the nearest one to fill the gaps), made mono,
trimmed, shortened with a fade, looped where the sound is held, evened out
in loudness, and saved as 112 kbps MP3. Each file is named for the note it
really sounds (the libraries disagree on whether middle C is C3 or C4).
